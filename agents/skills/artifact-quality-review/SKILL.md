# Artifact Quality Review

Review the exact exported deliverables, not only the generator source or an earlier preview.
Read [../professional-artifact-design/references/formats.md](../professional-artifact-design/references/formats.md) for the relevant format contract.

## Evidence and calculations

Trace material claims to source files or supplied evidence.
Recompute totals, percentages and chart values, and verify dates, units, denominators, filters and currency.
Ensure illustrative values are labelled at the point of use and implementation claims are not presented as live acceptance-test results.
Check that requested topics and formats are present and that the narrative is useful to the intended audience.

## Rendering and behaviour

Render every page/slide and inspect individual full-size views.
Check overflow, unintended overlap, text size, colour contrast, orphan headings, broken table rows, image legibility and consistent page furniture.
Use contact sheets to assess overall flow, not as a substitute for individual-page inspection.
Test contents links, bookmarks, hyperlinks, formulas, filters and interactions where present.
For a redesigned artifact, compare the final export with the original to confirm important content was preserved.

## Delivery gate

Correct defects within the authorised scope and re-export/re-render affected outputs.
Do not silently change source data or answer keys to make a chart or example look better.
State checks that could not run; structural validity does not prove visual correctness or formula recalculation.
Deliver only the requested final artifacts unless source files or previews were requested.
Summarise material validation limits separately from the audience-facing artifact when appropriate.
