# UX research and flows

## Clarify
- Who is the user, what job are they doing, what does success look like, what are the constraints and edge cases?
- Mark facts, assumptions and open questions separately. Ask the user before inventing research.

## Produce
1. Primary persona or user type (short, behaviour-based, no fiction padding).
2. Jobs to be done and top tasks, ranked.
3. User flow: entry, steps, decisions, error and empty paths, exit. Keep happy path under 5 steps where possible.
4. Information architecture: screens, navigation, naming.
5. Low-fidelity wireframes or a screen list, with states (loading, empty, error, success).

## Check
- Walk the flow as a first-time user; remove steps and fields that are not needed.
- Validate against heuristics (visibility of status, error prevention, consistency, recovery).
- Label any sample data as illustrative.
