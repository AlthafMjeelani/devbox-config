# Business Presentation

Compose with `professional-artifact-design`, `content-storytelling` and the installed presentation skill.
Determine whether the audience needs to learn, compare options, approve a decision or follow a demonstration.
Use that outcome to choose the narrative rather than starting with an inventory slide.

- Open with the business context and central message.
- Give each slide one job and a title that communicates its takeaway.
- Explain the significance of KPIs and evidence; a list of figures is not an insight.
- Use a process diagram for how the system works and a concrete question/result example for how it helps.
- Separate current functionality, conditional data coverage and future proposals.
- Use clearly labelled illustrative results when live data is unavailable.
- Put detailed inventories in the companion report or an appendix rather than shrinking them onto a slide.
- Include practical demonstration steps or a decision/action slide when it serves the purpose.
- Keep source notes and presenter detail in speaker notes where supported.

Adapt supplied branding and preserve editable charts/diagrams when possible.
Render and review every exported slide, including the opening, closing and any linked navigation.
